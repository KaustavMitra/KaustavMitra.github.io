I work with Prof. [Frank van den Bosch](https://campuspress.yale.edu/vdbosch/) on inferring galaxy-halo connection using Bayesian hierarchical modelling of satellite galaxy kinematics. The goal is to extract maximal information from the smallest and most non-linear scales in galaxy redshift survey data, to constrain galaxy formation models and cosmological parameters.
